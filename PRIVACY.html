Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Data collection

@mapper does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. No analytics, no telemetry, no remote servers.

Data storage

All data stays on your device using the browser's built-in extension storage:

  • Local storage. Your saved mappings, stored as shortcut and replacement pairs.
  • Sync storage. Your settings, which are the trigger character, theme, and case sensitivity, plus per-site enable and disable overrides. If you have browser sync on, your browser may sync this data to your other devices through your own browser account. @mapper has no access to that sync.

Permissions

  • storage. Reads and writes your mappings and settings.
  • <all_urls>. The content script runs on all pages so the autocomplete menu works in any text field. The extension does not read page content, cookies, or form data outside the field you are typing in.

Third parties

None. The extension has no third-party dependencies, no remote code, and no network requests.
